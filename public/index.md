## About

Riddhi More is an AI Solutions Architect in Toronto. She designs systems that connect language models with business data, APIs, and everyday workflows.

Her experience spans enterprise engineering, NLP research, and teaching. At Amgen, she built agents for Legal, Finance, and Pharmacovigilance. Today, she works on AI architecture and automation at Northeast Shared Services.

Her work focuses on making technology easier to understand and use. That perspective also shapes her research on efficient, reliable AI and her experience mentoring more than 500 students at Ontario Tech University.

## How Riddhi builds AI solutions

### 1. Requirements

Define the business problem, user workflow, constraints, and success criteria.

### 2. Prototype

Test models, retrieval, and tool integrations with a focused working prototype.

### 3. Evaluate

Measure answer quality and reliability. Check failure cases, groundedness, and data leakage.

### 4. Design

Plan architecture, access controls, integrations, observability, and long-term support.

### 5. Deliver

Deploy, document, measure usage, and improve the system with user feedback.

### ALIGNFREEZE: Multilingual Model Realignment Analysis

**Category:** Research  
**Context:** Multilingual NLP collaboration · NAACL 2025  
**Technologies:** Python, PyTorch, Transformers, multilingual NLP, BERT, XLM-R

Studied layer-wise changes during multilingual model realignment across three models, 35 languages, and four tasks. The work found that lower layers were particularly vulnerable to realignment and that freezing them could preserve performance when full realignment caused degradation.

- [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12959)

### HumanEval Data Leakage Mitigation Framework

**Category:** Research  
**Context:** SEER Lab · ICST 2025 short papers  
**Technologies:** Python, LLM evaluation, combinatorial testing, code generation

Co-authored HumanEval_T, which uses reusable task templates and combinatorial test design to generate fresh code-generation tasks. The method reduces reliance on a fixed benchmark whose tasks and solutions may have appeared in model training data.

- [Paper](https://www.sqrlab.ca/publications/icst2025_humaneval_t/)

### Assessing Data Augmentation-Induced Bias

**Category:** Research  
**Context:** SEER Lab · FAIRNESS 2025  
**Technologies:** Python, machine learning, data augmentation, model evaluation

Studied how augmented training and testing samples affect model evaluation, including SMOTE and mutation-based augmentation. The work highlights the need to examine dataset construction when interpreting performance.

- [Paper](https://www.sqrlab.ca/publications/fairness2025/)
