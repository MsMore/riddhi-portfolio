# Interview demonstration additions

Brand Compass uses newly generated fictional data: 60 monthly unit-volume observations and 2,369,871 daily treatment events across five years with 64 columns. The clinical Excel workbook contains a labeled 3,000-row sample; full calendar-year CSV.gz partitions reconcile to the monthly aggregates. Fictional product, patient, professional, site and region identifiers do not represent real people or clinical guidance. No Amgen code, internal documents, commercial results or patient records are reproduced. Dose values use fictional demonstration units.

Ridge models are selected using chronological expanding-window validation before the held-out year. The dashboard distinguishes a rolling one-step holdout evaluation, a fixed-origin recursive unseen-quarter evaluation, and six future months using stated clinical-input assumptions. Lag relationships and model coefficients are predictive associations, not causal findings. Forecast bands use historical error and are not calibrated prediction intervals. Metrics describe this synthetic prototype only.

Browser inference uses [WebLLM](https://webllm.mlc.ai/docs/user/basic_usage.html) and its prebuilt Qwen3 4B model catalog. Weights/runtime assets are fetched from the model/runtime URLs in the pinned SDK catalog after explicit initialization. The model plans bounded read-only analytical calls and writes a structured answer; deterministic functions produce chart values.
